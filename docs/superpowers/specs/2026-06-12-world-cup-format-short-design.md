# World Cup Format YouTube Short Design

## Goal

Produce an original English-language YouTube Short explaining the 2026 FIFA
World Cup format in about 35 seconds. The video should maximize retention and
comments while avoiding copyrighted match footage and FIFA branding.

## Deliverable

- MP4 video, 1080x1920, 9:16.
- Target duration: 33-35 seconds.
- Male English voice-over with energetic sports-news delivery.
- Large English kinetic captions.
- Original motion graphics and generated or royalty-free stadium atmosphere.
- No match highlights, broadcast clips, FIFA logo, or third-party watermarks.

## Production Approach

Use a hybrid workflow:

1. Generate atmospheric stadium, crowd, flags, and map backgrounds.
2. Build all numbers, tables, group labels, and captions as deterministic
   motion graphics so they remain readable and factually accurate.
3. Generate the voice-over separately and edit visuals to its final timing.
4. Add licensed or generated music, crowd ambience, bass hits, risers, and
   interface-style transitions.

This approach combines football atmosphere with reliable typography and avoids
the text distortions common in fully generated video.

## Visual Direction

The approved direction is **Broadcast Energy**:

- Deep navy and electric blue backgrounds.
- Red angled sports-news panels.
- Yellow number highlights.
- White condensed uppercase captions.
- Fast push-ins, ticker wipes, score flashes, and restrained camera shake.
- Balanced pacing: major compositions change every 3-5 seconds, with smaller
  motion accents between them.

The frame must keep critical text inside the central safe area so YouTube
interface controls do not obscure it.

## Voice-over

Use this fact-checked script:

> The World Cup is not the same anymore.
>
> For the first time, we have 48 teams instead of 32.
>
> That means 12 groups, four teams in each group, and here's the crazy part:
>
> You don't even need to finish top two to survive.
>
> The eight best third-place teams can still reach the knockout stage.
>
> So now, one goal difference, one late goal, even fair-play points from yellow
> cards could decide everything.
>
> This World Cup is going to be more chaotic than ever.
>
> So tell me: is the new format better, or did FIFA ruin it?

Delivery should be brisk but intelligible, with short pauses after `32`,
`crazy part`, and `everything`. If the generated read exceeds 35 seconds,
remove "from yellow cards" before increasing playback speed.

## Caption Rules

- Use short impact phrases rather than full sentences.
- Show no more than three lines at once.
- Keep captions large enough for a phone screen.
- Highlight numbers and decisive words in yellow.
- Use quick scale or slide animation without excessive bouncing.

Primary caption set:

- `WORLD CUP CHANGED`
- `48 TEAMS`
- `12 GROUPS`
- `THIRD PLACE CAN QUALIFY`
- `ONE GOAL MATTERS`
- `PURE CHAOS`
- `BETTER OR RUINED?`

## Audio

- Male English voice, energetic sports-news tone.
- Dramatic modern sports bed at low volume under narration.
- Bass hit at 0:00 and 0:02.
- Short whooshes for panel transitions.
- Crowd ambience introduced lightly at 0:25.
- No recognizable copyrighted song, commentary, or broadcast audio.

The voice must remain clearly louder than music and effects.

## Accuracy And Rights

- State that 48 teams are divided into 12 groups of four.
- State that the top two teams plus the eight best third-place teams advance.
- Describe yellow cards as potentially relevant through fair-play points, not
  as an earlier tie-breaker than points or goal difference.
- Use neutral flag imagery and original graphics.
- Do not imply official FIFA sponsorship or use protected broadcast visuals.
